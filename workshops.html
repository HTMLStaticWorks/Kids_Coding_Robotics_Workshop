How It Works

Cohort Learning Structure & Weekly Cadence

A structured balance of live instructor modeling, active hands-on lab sprints, and peer demonstration.

01

Interactive Masterclass (45m)

Mentors introduce core computational concepts through live code simulations, visual debugging demos, and interactive quiz challenges.

02

Guided Lab Sprint (45m)

Cadets build projects hands-on in small breakout groups of 6, receiving instant real-time instructor feedback and pair-coding assistance.

03

Hardware Testing & Play

Cadets upload scripts to their physical microcontrollers, calibrate ultrasonic sensors, and test rover movement on custom tracks.

04

Showcase & Trophy Unlock

Students publish their game or robot demo to the academy showcase gallery, unlocking digital achievement badges in their student portal.
