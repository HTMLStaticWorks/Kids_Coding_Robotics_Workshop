How Projects Are Built

The 4-Stage Student Engineering Lifecycle

Every showcase project goes through an authentic engineering design and code review cycle.

Step 1

Concept Sketch & Logic Map

Students diagram their game mechanics or robot behaviors on digital whiteboards before writing a single line of code.

Step 2

Hardware & Circuit Assembly

Cadets mount chassis plates, connect ultrasonic pins, wire LED status indicators, and test electrical continuity.

Step 3

Code Sprints & Unit Testing

Writing the main execution loop, implementing obstacle response logic, and testing edge cases on physical arena tracks.

Step 4

Peer Demo & Gallery Release

Students record a live video walkthrough of their build and publish their repository to their permanent student portfolio.
