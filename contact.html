Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our courses, kits, and schedule.

Do kids need prior coding experience?

No prior experience is needed! Our beginner Scratch tracks start with drag-and-drop logic puzzles suitable for absolute beginners.

Are hardware kits included in the tuition?

Yes, all required microcontrollers, sensors, and robotics components are shipped directly to your home before the first day of class.

What happens if my child misses a lesson?

All lessons are recorded and accessible anytime on the Student Portal, complete with instructor notes and interactive sandbox exercises.
